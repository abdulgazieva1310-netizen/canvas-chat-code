tests cover both outcomes. The role-specific live failure predates this fix;
its exact rejected provider text was not retained, so the fix does not establish
that every live failure is resolved.

live content failures remain, human semantic review is pending, and physical
microphone/listening checks have not been run. The validator always records
`releaseApproved: false`. Follow the review checklist before promoting Russian
or another provider/browser combination.
