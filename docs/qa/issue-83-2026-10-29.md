exhaust malformed input or near-limit performance cases; the spec documents
these as new-version review work. Existing reference programs must also be
reviewed against any future added cases. Production judge budgets, container
limits and exact stdout formatting still apply. This change does not alter
published cases, saved scores or sandbox isolation behavior.
